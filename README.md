# Woodshed

A browser-based practice companion for monophonic instruments. It listens
through your microphone, scores each note you play for intonation and timing,
and keeps a long-term record of the specific notes, intervals and transitions
you get wrong — then keeps putting them in front of you.

Local-first: audio never leaves your device, no account, works offline.

**Status: planning.** Nothing is built yet. See [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) for the full
proposal — scope, architecture, scoring model, milestones and risks.
